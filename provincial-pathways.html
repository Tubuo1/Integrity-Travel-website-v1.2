Provincial pathways

Provincial nomination: opportunity with province-specific rules.

Canada’s provinces and territories operate immigration streams that respond to their own economic and demographic needs. Some connect to Express Entry; others use different processes.

Explore your optionsBook a consultation
What this means

One country, many provincial systems.

A provincial nomination can be significant, but there is no single “PNP.” Each province decides which streams it offers, who can apply and when. Never rely on an old list of occupations or an outdated screenshot.

01

Express Entry-linked streams

Some provincial nominations are connected to Express Entry. A qualifying nomination can add 600 CRS points.

02

Base / non-Express Entry streams

Other provincial routes operate outside the Express Entry pool and have their own application sequence.

03

Province-specific criteria

Work experience, job offers, language, education, ties or other factors may matter depending on the stream.

Monitoring

Treat provincial programs as moving targets

Streams can pause, reopen, change quotas, change eligible occupations or alter selection methods. Our website deliberately avoids hard-coding details that are likely to become stale.

Decision

A nomination is not something to buy

Be cautious if anyone sells a nomination, guarantees a province will select you, or asks you to misrepresent where you intend to live. Provincial immigration involves legal declarations and government decisions.
