What this means

A real job is not a product we sell.

Integrity does not sell “guaranteed Canadian jobs.” We do not ask clients to pay us for employment. Where career support is offered, it focuses on readiness, positioning and understanding legitimate pathways.

Work authorization

Canada

Work permits have different legal bases

Canada has employer-specific and open work permits, but open work permits are available only in particular situations. Never assume a visitor can simply “convert” to work status because someone promises a job.

Fraud

Red flags in international recruitment

  • A guaranteed job after paying an agent
  • Pressure to send money to a personal account immediately
  • A contract with unverifiable company details
  • A recruiter who discourages you from contacting the employer directly
  • Requests to lie about duties, salary or experience

What we can help you prepare
