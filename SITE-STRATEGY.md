# Integrity Travel & Consulting - Website Strategy & Launch Handoff

**Brand:** Integrity Travel & Consulting  
**Slogan:** *Where Dreams Take Flight*  
**Primary positioning:** Do not begin with an application. Begin with a strategy.

## Brand positioning
Integrity Travel & Consulting is a bilingual, African-aware travel and global-mobility brand that helps professionals, students and families replace rumours and pressure with clear information, careful preparation and realistic next steps. Canada is the initial flagship destination, with Express Entry and permanent-residence preparation as major content pillars, while the brand remains broad enough to expand into education, work, travel and wider international mobility.

## Primary audiences
1. Skilled professionals in Cameroon and across Africa considering Canadian permanent residence.
2. Francophone and bilingual candidates who may benefit from understanding the role of verified French and English results.
3. Students and families evaluating international education.
4. Jobseekers exploring legitimate international career opportunities.
5. Travellers planning family visits, tourism, business travel and international itineraries.
6. African diaspora clients who want structured guidance for relatives or their own future mobility decisions.

## Audience pain points
- Conflicting immigration information and old social-media advice.
- Not knowing what to do first: language test, ECA, documents or profile.
- Fake agents, fake job offers and unrealistic guarantees.
- Difficulty understanding government terminology.
- Document retrieval and credential-evaluation challenges from Africa.
- International payments, test-centre access and time-zone friction.
- Family pressure and fear of wasting savings.
- Confusion between admission, work authorization and permanent residence.

## Value proposition
**Clear information. Careful preparation. Honest strategy.** Integrity helps clients understand the journey, prepare in the right order and know when regulated professional support is required. The company does not sell outcomes it cannot control.

## Differentiators
- Preparation before application.
- Strong anti-fraud and verification position.
- English/French experience from launch.
- African-market relevance without stereotyping.
- Official sources first and visible review dates for changing immigration information.
- No fabricated reviews, success rates or partnerships.
- Explicit separation between general planning/information and regulated Canadian immigration advice/representation.
- Free resources before paid conversion.

## Visual direction implemented
- Deep navy + controlled red accent from the supplied logo.
- White space and soft neutral surfaces.
- Minimal aviation motif through shape, movement and language rather than passport/airplane stock-photo clichés.
- Logo used directly in header/footer.
- Typography uses a clean system sans-serif stack for fast loading and licensing simplicity.

## Quality and compliance rule
No publishable page should invent a licence, success rate, client count, office, partnership, job, testimonial, award or government connection. Dynamic immigration rules should be verified on official sources and dated when discussed in detail.
