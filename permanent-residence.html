Permanent residence

Permanent residence starts with finding the right pathway — not forcing the wrong one.

Canada has several economic and family immigration routes. The correct pathway depends on current rules and your circumstances. We help you understand the landscape and prepare questions and evidence in the right order.

What this means

One goal. Different legal pathways.

“Permanent residence” is the outcome many people want, but it is not one single application. Express Entry, provincial nomination and other programs have distinct eligibility rules, documents and timelines.

01

Express Entry

Federal skilled-worker immigration managed through an online pool and invitation system.

02

Provincial nomination

Programs run by provinces and territories, some linked to Express Entry and some outside it.

03

Other pathways

Family, regional or other programs may exist depending on current law and personal circumstances.

Start with fit

Do not choose a program because someone else succeeded with it

A colleague, sibling or TikTok creator may have a completely different age, language score, education, occupation, family profile or immigration history. The first task is to identify which official pathways are actually relevant to you.

Program availability and criteria change. Use current official sources and, where individualized legal advice is needed, an authorized representative.
Preparation

What strong preparation looks like

  • Accurate identity and civil-status documents
  • Verified education and work-history information
  • Language results where required
  • Financial planning and proof where required
  • Consistent disclosure across forms and supporting documents
  • A plan for deadlines and document validity
Family

A family application is a family strategy

Spouses, partners and dependent children can affect documentation, costs, timing and sometimes the strategic choice of principal applicant. Plan as a household, not as an afterthought.
