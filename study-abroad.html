Canada

Do not assume every school or program leads to the same post-study options

Canada maintains a list of designated learning institutions (DLIs), and post-graduation work permit eligibility has specific rules that can change. Verify the exact institution and program before making a major financial commitment.

Admissions

Admission is not a visa

A school can admit you, but immigration authorities decide study-permit applications. Likewise, a study permit does not guarantee future permanent residence. Build each stage on its own requirements.

Parents & families

Plan the whole financial picture

For families, a study plan can affect household budgets, childcare, accompanying family members, housing and long-term decisions. Build a realistic scenario before committing funds.
