French can matter

Francophone or bilingual? Treat language as part of the strategy.

Canada’s Express Entry system includes a French-language proficiency category. Strong French can be strategically relevant for some candidates, but language alone never guarantees an invitation or permanent residence.

Learn about language planning

A useful question is not “Does French guarantee PR?”

A better question is: “Given my full profile, what role could stronger English, stronger French, or both play in my preparation?”
