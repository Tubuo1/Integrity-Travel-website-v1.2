Prepare for the meeting

Bring facts, not a suitcase of documents.

  • Your main goal and preferred destination.
  • Your current country of residence and citizenship.
  • Education and language-test information, if relevant.
  • Work history and any previous refusals, if relevant.
  • A short list of the questions you most need answered.

Do not send highly sensitive medical, criminal, banking or identity documents through the public contact form.
